What I do

Leadership. Build and lead AI organizations, owning strategy, product, and the client, investor, and partner relationships. For example, grew Tickr's AI and AI-engineering function from founding scientist to fifteen.
Agents & post-training. Post-train and adapt open-weight LLMs, build agentic systems, and keep them fast through evaluation and inference optimization. For example, a 4B model post-trained to cut errors roughly 50% versus GPT-5.2 at nearly 20× lower cost without sacrificing p95 latency.
Legal AI. Lead legal-AI strategy, stay hands-on with the modeling, and mentor the engineers building systems that read legal process and draft grounded output. For example, raised a legal-process classifier from 78.0% to 97.6% with DSPy, MIPROv2, and Bayesian optimization.
(Agentic) risk intelligence. Build agentic systems that surface latent and emerging risk from messy public and licensed data. For example, built a verifiable-reward loop that mines predictive signals, feeding a model that predicts multidistrict litigation at 0.90 held-out AUC (2025).
Predictive modeling & forecasting. Build forecasting and causal-inference pipelines, increasingly AI-assisted, across financial services and consumer goods. For example, Generative Predictor Search cut MAPE 15.6% at 13× the speed of brute-force search while mitigating spurious correlations.
AI for science. Use AI, deep learning, and agents to advance scientific measurement and discovery. For example, first author on NEO (featured by NVIDIA) and co-author on a Nature Portfolio paper.
Athletics. Passionate about time in nature, and sometimes fiercely competitive about it. For example, the youngest open-class sailing world champion ever, two Transpacific Yacht Race wins, the US Sailing Team, and multiple world-championship podiums, all as skipper.
