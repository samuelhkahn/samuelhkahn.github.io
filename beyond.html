Sailing

I raced through my teens and early twenties, across one-design classes and offshore. In 2003, at fourteen, I became the youngest person ever to win an open-class sailing world championship. In 2005 I qualified for the United States Sailing Team.

Sailing photographs courtesy of Pegasus Racing.

Astrophotography

I shoot deep-sky targets and wide nightscapes, mostly across the Eastern Sierra and on expeditions abroad. Same instinct as the day job: pull faint signal out of a noisy sky and make it legible.
